# LEGO SPIKE Prime Wiring Diagram

## Motor Connections

| SPIKE Prime Hub Port | Component |
|---|---|
| Port A | Large Angular Motor 1 |
| Port B | Large Angular Motor 2 |
| Port C | Medium Angular Motor |

## Power

The SPIKE Prime Hub contains the rechargeable battery and provides power to the connected motors through the SPIKE Prime motor cables.

No external power supply or PCB is used.

## Wiring

SPIKE Prime Hub
- Port A → Large Angular Motor 1
- Port B → Large Angular Motor 2
- Port C → Medium Angular Motor
